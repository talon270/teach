# Teach, Monsoon 2026
Lessons, reference pages, drills and notes for each course. Open `index.html` (landing page) or `plan.html` (day plan).
The landing page is a progress dashboard: next test, day-plan items done by today, lessons finished, study minutes over 7 days, days fully on plan, today's tasks, a 14-day minutes chart, recently opened pages, and per course a progress bar with a status for each lesson. A tick there is the same tick as in the day plan (one `localStorage` key). The day plan folds the days before today and marks today.
Tracking is automatic and stays in the browser (`localStorage` key `teach-track-v1`). `build.py` adds a small script to every published page that records visits, active minutes (tab visible, input in the last 3 minutes) and each finished lesson quiz with its score and date. A day-plan item ticks itself when its `auto` rule in `plan.py` is met (lesson quiz finished; RECALL quiz redone on or after its day; INT301 recall by any INT301 quiz or 5 minutes of INT301 notes). An item ticked or unticked by hand is never changed. "Save a copy" and "Load a copy" on the dashboard move the records to another browser; loading merges and never removes anything.
Every page except the landing page has a bar at the top and the bottom: Back (the previous page, or one level up when you opened the page directly), the course map, and All courses. `build.py` step 6 adds it at publish time; the source pages stay untouched.
Test dates live in `EXAMS` in `study/_context/build/teach/plan.py`; add the other exams there when their dates are known.
Layout: `<COURSE>/` for lessons, `notes/<COURSE>/` for study notes and drills.
The pages are mirrored from the local study folder by `publish.sh`, which also rebuilds `index.html`, pushes to GitHub and deploys to Vercel (https://teach-three-blond.vercel.app/). `.vercelignore` keeps the build files off the site. Do not edit files here by hand.
Course PDFs and readings are not published.
