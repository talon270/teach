2 · Essay practice

"Nuclear order has served the powerful at the expense of justice." Outline five points.

Bull: order's mechanisms "systematically affront" justice; the balance of terror. → Higuchi/Todeschini: powers defined harm, victims unrecognised. → Roehrlich/Hecht: IAEA shaped by North–South fights; "atomic colonialism"; nuclearity negotiated by apartheid South Africa. → Counter: Tannenwald's taboo and Gibbons's TPNW show norms and smaller states pushing back. → Ritchie: two incommensurable ontologies; the contest continues.

"Why have nuclear weapons not been used since 1945?" Outline four positions.

Deterrence: Waltz (cost-sensitive leaders), the panel (force checking force). → Limits: McNamara (no military use), Powell (theory claims too much), Sagan (organizations may fail). → Norm: Tannenwald (taboo; anomalies; permissive effect). → Constructed confidence: Fraise (the script of deterrence), Connelly (forecasting kept it cold).
