Course map

Eleven short lessons that assume you know nothing about nuclear history or strategy. They follow the course in order: order vs justice, the first attempts at control, the bomb's victims, Atoms for Peace, the atomic everyday, then strategy, proliferation, the taboo, disarmament and feminist critiques. Each lesson asks the course's three questions: whose order, which actors, what counts as "nuclear".
