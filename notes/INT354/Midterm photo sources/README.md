# INT354 midterm: source photographs

Five original-resolution historical photographs for the proposed theme of nuclear non-use, military utility, and the nuclear taboo. They are candidates for a photo essay, not a finished sequence. Their subjects come from the assigned [McNamara (1983)](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/articles/1983-09-01/military-role-nuclear-weapons-perceptions-and-misperceptions) and [Tannenwald (1999)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-organization/article/nuclear-taboo-the-united-states-and-the-normative-basis-of-nuclear-nonuse/EA04E0104A42C12FC785A70F301197CC) readings. These photographs do not appear in those readings.

## Caption discipline

- The Pershing photograph shows a missile stage being handled, not a launch or an armed warhead.
- The F-117 photograph shows a takeoff. Its target and the outcome of that mission are not identified by the image.
- The highway photograph shows abandoned vehicles. It cannot by itself establish casualties or illustrate the specific infrastructure attacks Tannenwald discusses.
- The protest photograph documents opposition to a NATO deployment. It does not by itself establish why a government did or did not use nuclear weapons.

Credit the photographer or institution and archive identifier under any photograph used in the submission.
