Course map

Fourteen short lessons that assume you know nothing about international law. They build the vocabulary first (sources, natural law vs positivism), then walk through every reading in the course up to week 11, and include essay frames for the end-sem (lesson 12). Each lesson also drills the "who argued what" facts the surprise quizzes test. End-sem scope (19 Nov): the syllabus starts at week 4, Kourtis, "A Racialized Existence". Lessons 3, 4 and 7–14 are on it, including lessons 13 (week 10) and 14 (week 11); lessons 1, 2, 5 and 6 (weeks 1–3) are not. Lessons 13 and 14 come after the synthesis in this list; do them before it.
