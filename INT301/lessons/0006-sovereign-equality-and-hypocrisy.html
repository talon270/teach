Equality and hypocrisy

The UN Charter says all states are sovereign equals. Two week-2 readings ask why that formal equality sits beside deep real inequality, and what the gap produces: racial hierarchy (Mbori) and constant charges of hypocrisy (Knox).

Your win todayExplain "formal vs material equality" with one example from each reading.

1 · Warm-up from lesson 5

Name Chimni's two logics and his three post-1945 strategies.

Logic of capital vs logic of territory. Strategies: power, mediation, revolution.

2 · Mbori: the inequalities of sovereign equality

  • Westphalia (1648) is a "heuristic myth" behind a linear story of sovereignty. Through TWAIL, sovereignty is fluid and contested.
  • Sovereign equality propelled racial capitalism through its "racialized grammar" and "abstract liberalism", structuring the world along the global colour line: Europeans sovereign, non-Europeans not (discovery, terra nullius, unequal treaties).
  • Enacted through slavery, colonialism and the mandate and trusteeship systems (A/B/C "racist categories").
  • Re-enacted today in the UN Security Council veto, Bretton Woods weighted voting and the developed/developing paradigm.

What does Mbori call Westphalia?

A "heuristic myth" that props up a linear story of sovereignty.

3 · Knox: imperialism, hypocrisy and IL

  • Lawyers dismiss "hypocrisy" as mere rhetoric. Knox: accusations of hypocrisy are central to IL.
  • Opening case: Crimea 2014. Russia claimed self-determination there but opposed it for Kosovo; the General Assembly declared the referendum invalid.
  • Political theory: with no agreed values, hypocrisy becomes political currency; Krasner's "organized hypocrisy" of sovereignty.
  • The deeper cause (Marxist): the contradiction between formal legal equality and social and economic inequality under capitalism keeps generating hypocrisy.
  • Traced from the "New World" (Inter caetera 1493, Tordesillas 1494) through the standard of civilisation, to Ukraine 2022: Putin's "lies and hypocrisy"; 35 abstentions on the GA condemnation; Riad Malki's "Amazing hypocrisy".

Whose phrase is "organized hypocrisy"?

Stephen Krasner, on sovereignty. Knox builds on it.
