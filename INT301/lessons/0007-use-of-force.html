The use of force

Until 1928, war was a lawful way to settle disputes: "might made right". Then states outlawed it. Did that work? Hathaway & Shapiro say yes, until now. Kourtis shows how law can also prepare the ground for mass violence.

Your win todayExplain outcasting, and Kourtis's "primed" and "activated", in one sentence each.

1 · Warm-up from lesson 6

What does Knox say causes hypocrisy in IL?

The contradiction between formal legal equality and social and economic inequality under capitalism.

2 · Hathaway & Shapiro: might unmakes right

  • Before 1928, war was lawful: might made right.
  • The Kellogg–Briand Pact (1928) outlawed war as national policy. The UN Charter Art 2(4) (1945) banned the threat or use of force. Conquest became illegal.
  • This, more than deterrence or democracy, produced eight decades of rare interstate war, freer trade, and sanctions-based "outcasting": violators are excluded from the benefits of cooperation, not attacked.
  • It rested on an expectation that violators would pay, especially under US leadership. Trump's threats (Greenland, the Panama Canal, Gaza, Ukraine) are a cohesive assault that destroys that expectation.
  • Remedy: midsize and small states, the General Assembly and new coalitions must share enforcement, while admitting the old system's flaws.

What is Hathaway & Shapiro's term for enforcing law by excluding violators from cooperation?

Outcasting: sanctions and exclusion instead of war.

3 · Kourtis: a racialized existence

  • The Genocide Convention (1948) protects national, ethnical, racial and religious groups. But reading "race" by visible traits, by the perpetrator's view, or by its 1940s meaning all fail.
  • Race is a dynamic, relational social construct produced by a "racial contract" that law mediates. A racial group's identity is "hybrid, situational, fragmentary, and negotiable".
  • Palestinians, from the Mandate through Israeli citizenship, land and security law, were racialized (tantamount to apartheid), which "primed" genocide. After 7 October 2023 the conditions were "activated".
  • So Palestinians qualify as a protected group.
