Financial investments

Some investments are held only for their returns. Two tests sort them into three boxes, and each box treats gains and interest differently.

1 · Warm-up from lesson 11

Write the goodwill formula. How is NCI measured for full goodwill?

Goodwill = consideration + NCI − fair value of net assets. Full: NCI = NCI shares × market price.

3 · Bond prices move opposite to yields

Price = present value of coupons + present value of face value, at the market yield. Yield > coupon → price below face (discount). Yield = coupon → at par. Yield < coupon → above face (premium). Semi-annual bonds: halve the rate, double the periods.

4 · The effective interest method

Interest income = opening carrying amount × effective rate (yield), not the coupon. Closing = opening + interest income − cash received.
