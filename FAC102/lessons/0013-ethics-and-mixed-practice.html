Ethics and mixed practice

The end-sem is mapped to three outcomes: identifying risks (PLO 3.3), ethical principles (PLO 6.1) and recommending corrective actions for compliance (PLO 6.2). So expect a scenario that asks: what is wrong, which principle does it break, and what should the company do?

Your win todayAnswer a scenario question in three lines, then pass a mixed quiz that jumps between topics.

1 · The three-line answer

  1. Risk: what is misstated, and which way (profit or assets too high or too low)?
  2. Principle: which rule is broken (recognition, matching, reporting entity, cost, conservatism, neutrality, comparability)?
  3. Corrective action: the right entry, plus a control (consistent policy, disclosure, cut-off review, audit committee).

3 · Try one

On 30 March a company ships goods FOB destination that arrive on 4 April, and books the sale in March to meet its target. Give the three-line answer.

Risk: March revenue and profit overstated. Principle: revenue recognition (ownership passes on arrival). Action: reverse the sale into April, keep the goods in March inventory, and add a cut-off review at year end.

A firm capitalises its routine machine maintenance. Three-line answer.

Risk: assets and profit overstated. Principle: only costs that bring the asset to use or extend its life are capital spending; routine repairs are expenses (matching). Action: expense them, restate, and set a written capitalisation policy.
