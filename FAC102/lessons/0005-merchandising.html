Buying and selling goods

A trading business earns its profit on the gap between what it pays for goods and what it sells them for. This lesson follows one sale from list price to net sales, then one purchase to cost of goods sold (COGS).

Your win todayWork out net sales and COGS, and say who owns goods in transit.

1 · Warm-up from lesson 4

Name the four timing differences, and say which ones create a liability.

Accrued revenue (asset), accrued expense (liability), deferred revenue (liability), deferred expense (asset).

Terms "2/10, n/45" mean: 2% off if paid within 10 days; otherwise the full amount within 45 days. Day 11 gets no discount. A return sends goods back; an allowance lets the buyer keep faulty goods at a lower price. Returns and discounts are contra-revenue accounts: they take a debit.

Invoice 60,000, terms 2/10, n/30. The buyer returns 10,000 and pays the rest on day 8. How much cash does the seller get?

Owed after the return: 50,000. 2% discount = 1,000. Cash = 49,000. The discount is on what is still owed, after returns.

3 · The buyer's side

Net purchases = Purchases − Purchase returns − Purchase discounts. Net cost of purchases = Net purchases + Freight-in.

Goods shipped FOB destination are destroyed on the way. Who bears the loss: buyer or seller?

The seller. Under FOB destination the seller owns the goods until they arrive.

5 · Perpetual vs periodic, and COGS

  • Perpetual: every purchase and sale updates Inventory; COGS is recorded at each sale. A count then reveals shrinkage (theft, damage).
  • Periodic: count the stock at period end and work out COGS once. Shrinkage hides inside COGS.

Goods available for sale = Opening inventory + Net cost of purchases.
COGS = Goods available − Closing inventory.
Gross profit = Net sales − COGS. Net profit = Gross profit − Operating expenses.

Opening inventory 12,000; purchases 60,000; purchase returns 3,000; freight-in 2,000; closing inventory 17,000. COGS?

Net cost of purchases = 60,000 − 3,000 + 2,000 = 59,000. Available = 71,000. COGS = 71,000 − 17,000 = 54,000.
